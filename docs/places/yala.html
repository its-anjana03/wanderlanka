This is leopard country

Three reasons it belongs on your route.

  1. 01

    Leopards are the top predator here, so they are bolder in daylight than in Africa or India.

  2. 02

    Elephants, sloth bears, crocodiles and hundreds of bird species share the same drive.

  3. 03

    The park runs down to an empty stretch of Indian Ocean beach.

How to see it

Go

Take the dawn drive. In the dry months animals gather at the water holes.

Know

The park usually closes for some weeks around September and October. Check before you plan around it.

Skip

Operators who race to sightings. Ask for a driver who keeps distance. For fewer jeeps, consider Wilpattu, or Udawalawe for elephants.

The story so far

This coast was farmed two thousand years ago as part of the ancient kingdom of Ruhuna. Ruined reservoirs and the rock monastery of Sithulpawwa are still inside the park.

Yala was made a wildlife sanctuary in 1900 and a national park in 1938. The 2004 tsunami came ashore here. A memorial at Patanangala remembers the visitors and staff who died.

Getting there

The usual base is Tissamaharama, about 2 hours from Ella and 3 hours from Galle. Jeeps leave before dawn.

Good manners here

  • Stay in the jeep, keep your voice low and never ask the driver to go closer.
