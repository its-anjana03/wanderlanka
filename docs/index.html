24 hours on the island

Every moment at the hour it is best, from the first climb to the full moon.

c.02:00Sri Pada

Climb through the night

In season, pilgrims start around two. The path is lit and tea stalls are open all the way up.

06:00Horton Plains

World's End, before the cloud

Be at the gate by six. The view is usually lost in cloud by about half past nine.

c.06:15Yala

The dawn drive

In the dry months animals gather at the water holes. Ask for a driver who keeps a respectful distance.

c.07:00Sigiriya

The gate opens

The rock is cooler and far emptier before half past eight.

c.08:30Breakfast

Hoppers and tea

Bowl-shaped pancakes of fermented rice batter, crisp at the edge and soft in the middle. Ask for an egg in one.

c.09:00Kandy to Ella

The train climbs into the tea

Six to seven hours from Kandy through the hill country. Sit on the right-hand side leaving Kandy.

c.12:30Lunch

Rice and curry

Not one dish but a table: rice with four to eight small curries, a sambol and a crisp. Lunch is the main meal.

c.16:30Polonnaruwa

Gal Vihara in late light

Cycle the loop from south to north and reach the four great Buddhas in the late afternoon.

c.17:00Jaffna

Nallur, late afternoon

Be at the temple for the late afternoon ceremony, then eat on the street outside.

c.17:45Galle Fort

The ramparts at sunset

Walk from the clock tower to the lighthouse in the hour before sunset.

c.18:30Kandy

Drums at the evening offering

The evening offering at the Temple of the Tooth. Arrive thirty minutes before.

c.19:30Dinner

Kottu on the street

Chopped flatbread fried on a hot plate. You hear the blades before you see the stall.

21:00Full moon

Full moon nights

Every full moon, Poya, is a public holiday. Temples fill with white-clad families and alcohol is not sold.
