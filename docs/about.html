How we check

Five rules for every page.

  1. Every changeable fact is dated.

    Prices, visa rules and phone numbers show when they were last checked and link to where they were read.

  2. Official first.

    A fact marked "official" was read on a government or site-authority page. One marked "guide" came from a travel guide because the official page did not state it.

  3. Old facts say so.

    After six months a fact turns amber. After twelve it turns red. It stays visible, with its date, so you can judge it.

  4. Legend is labelled.

    History is summarised from UNESCO listings and standard references. Stories from chronicles and local tradition are marked as such.

  5. Not yet reviewed locally.

    This first edition was researched from published sources. It has not yet been reviewed by archaeologists, guides or ritual specialists in Sri Lanka. Corrections are welcome.
